Home Doorbell Recorder

A personal app connecting my Nest doorbell to my home recorder.

What it does

Home Doorbell Recorder is a small, private, non-commercial app for one household. It uses Google's Smart Device Management API to receive the live video stream from the app owner's own Nest doorbell, so that a video recorder running on a computer at home can save short clips when someone arrives at the door.

Who it is for

Only the app owner. It is not offered to the public, there is nothing to sign up for, and it does not access anyone else's devices or accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app reads the live video from the one doorbell the owner chose to share. It does not control the doorbell or change any settings.

Privacy policy

Home Doorbell Recorder is a personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
