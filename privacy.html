Privacy Policy

Home Doorbell Recorder · Effective October 23, 2026

Home Doorbell Recorder is a personal app that connects the app owner's own Nest doorbell to a video recorder at home. It is used only by the app owner.

What information the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google and chooses which device to share, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the live video and audio stream from that one Nest doorbell. It does not access any other Google data, such as email, contacts, files, or location, and it does not access anyone else's devices.

How the information is used

The stream is used only so that the home recorder can show live video and save short clips when a person, vehicle, or animal is detected at the door.

Where the information is stored

Recordings and snapshots are stored only on the owner's own computer at home and are deleted automatically after a limited number of days. The sign-in credentials the app needs are kept in a private file on that same computer.

Sharing

The information is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not uploaded to any server other than Google's own service that provides the stream.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Home Doorbell Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or from the Nest partner connections settings. Withdrawing access stops the stream, and stored recordings can be deleted from the home computer.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through the support email listed on the Google sign-in screen.
